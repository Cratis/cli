## Added

- `cratis render` accepts Screenplay ESM v17 models: `then no events` renders for commands and single production-only reactions, and `when redelivered <Event> to <Reaction>` renders for a single named reaction trigger with event productions or one supported command invocation. Fan-out, deeper cascades and other shapes Stage cannot render yet are refused with `STAGE-ESM-011`. (#365)
- `cratis render` accepts Screenplay ESM v19 models: reaction invocations can declare `on refused`, `on refused by validation`, `on refused by constraint [Name]` and `on refused by authorization` branches that `produce` events from `$refusal.reason`, `$refusal.constraint` and `$refusal.message` or `acknowledge` the refusal. Shapes Stage cannot reproduce exactly are refused with `STAGE-ESM-039`. Models newer than v19 are refused with `CLI-RENDER-004`. (#365)

## Changed

- The CLI bundles Stage 4.103.0 and Screenplay 4.160.0; rendered applications use Chronicle 19.64.1 and Arc 22.88.0, and `cratis run` starts the `cratis/stage:4.103.0` image.
