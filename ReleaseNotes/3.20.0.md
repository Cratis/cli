## Changed

- The CLI now bundles Screenplay 4.37.0 and Stage 4.18.1, and `cratis run` defaults to the matching Stage image.

## Fixed

- `cratis render` explains why a model whose events declare several generations cannot be rendered yet: alongside Stage's `STAGE-ESM-016`, it reports `CLI-RENDER-003` naming the evolved event. The command fails with a validation error and writes nothing. Screenplay itself validates such models; Stage does not yet accept their executable model (ESM v4).
