## Changed

- `cratis render --scope` no longer stops on execution-plan problems (`PLAN-*`) in slices outside the scope: they are reported as warnings naming the declaration, and only problems in the selected slices or application-level declarations block. A scope whose slices were all removed still re-renders to clean up its files. The CLI uses Screenplay 4.134.0 (#247)
- `cratis render` refuses models whose navigation contributions declare `id`, `icon`, `presentation`, `group` or `destination` with `CLI-RENDER-015`, instead of rendering without them, until the bundled Stage renderer supports them (#247)
