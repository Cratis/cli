`cratis render` and `cratis run` now use Stage 4.67.0 and the bundled Screenplay compiler is 4.131.0. Guarded screen actions that earlier releases removed from the rendered application are now rendered and run.

## Added

- A guarded screen action the Stage runtime can evaluate, such as `Close` shown only while `item.status == "open"`, is rendered with its guard and its hidden fallback instead of being removed. Only a guarded action the runtime cannot evaluate is left out, and it is reported as a `STAGE-SCENE-ACTION-001` warning. (part of #284)
- The rendered application's menu and breadcrumb follow the authored navigation hierarchy, and `cratis run` starts `cratis/stage:4.67.0` by default. (part of #284)

## Changed

- The CLI bundles Stage 4.67.0 and Screenplay 4.131.0. (part of #284)
