## Added

- `cratis chronicle constraints reindex` rebuilds constraint indexes from the event log for an event sequence (all unique and closing constraints, or the named ones). `--wait` follows the started job until it finishes and reports success, failure or an indeterminate outcome with its job ID (#603)

## Fixed

- `cratis chronicle observers fast-forward`, `clear-partition-quarantine` and `retry-partition` report success when Chronicle 19.196.0 or newer accepts the operation for background processing
