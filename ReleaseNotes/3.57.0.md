## Summary

`cratis render` now renders models that use Screenplay query filters (ESM v14) and concept generation strategies (ESM v15). The CLI bundles Stage 4.89.1 and Screenplay 4.147.1.

## Changed

- The CLI bundles Stage 4.89.1 and Screenplay 4.147.1, and `cratis run` starts the matching `cratis/stage:4.89.1` image. (#345)
- `cratis render` admits ESM v14 and v15: equality query filters render on observable collection queries, and concepts with a generation strategy (UUID v4/v7, ULID, TypeID, NanoID, random bytes) render with freshness scopes on generated event sources. Models newer than v15 are still refused with `CLI-RENDER-004`. (#345)
- Reactions declaring `runs as` and reactions with a `from` observer filter now render through the bundled Stage instead of being refused with `STAGE-ESM-034`/`STAGE-ESM-035`; `STAGE-ESM-034` remains only for system-identity reactions that invoke commands whose authorization reads claims or uses opaque policies. (#345)
