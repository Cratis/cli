## Added

- `cratis screenplay validate --check <names>` runs Screenplay's opt-in structural completeness checks (`data-bindings`, `input-surfaces`, `field-origins`, `query-keys`, `event-consumers`, `navigation`, the codes `PLAY0530`–`PLAY0537`, or `all`). The option can be repeated, its findings are warnings that fail only with `--warnings-as-errors`, and the checks are skipped with a note when the model has errors. It combines with `--executable`. (part of #274)

## Changed

- The `cratis screenplay validate` JSON summary reports `valid`, `errors` and `warnings`, and with `--check` it also reports `checks`, `completenessStatus` and, when the checks were skipped, `completenessNote`.
