## Changed

- The documentation now states that a corpus extension may own additional top-level sections of `.cratis/ai.json`, and `cratis ai install` and `update` keep them unchanged. (#208)

## Fixed

- The update hint for a dotnet tool installation picks the highest stable version the NuGet index lists instead of relying on the order of the list, so a reordered index no longer produces a stale or wrong hint. (#203)
