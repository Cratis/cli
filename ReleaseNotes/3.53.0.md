The CLI renders and runs the navigation hierarchy, toolbars, outlets and dialogs a model authors: it bundles Stage 4.73.0, which hosts them through Scene's navigation host, and no longer refuses the navigation metadata Stage could not honor before.

## Changed

- Bundles Stage 4.73.0 (Scene 4.21.0, Screenplay 4.137.0) and runs `cratis/stage:4.73.0` by default. The release brings module-level and screen-level navigation contributions, a screen's place in the module and feature hierarchy, toolbar items that open their destination, presentation hints (`icon`, `text`, `both`), and the Scene host's outlets, dialogs and deep links. (part of #284)

## Removed

- The `CLI-RENDER-015` refusal of navigation contributions that declare `id`, `icon`, `presentation`, `group` or `destination outlet|dialog|external`: Stage 4.73.0 honours them, so `cratis render` now renders them and writes them into the scene. (part of #284)
