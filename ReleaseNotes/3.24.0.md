## Added

- `cratis chronicle observers clear-partition-quarantine <observer> <partition>` clears the quarantine of a single failed partition that exhausted its retry attempts, resets its retry budget and starts the retry; `--no-retry` only clears. It needs Chronicle 19.29.0 or later and says so when the server does not support it. (#223)

## Changed

- `observers retry-partition` now points at `clear-partition-quarantine` when the partition is quarantined, and `observers clear-quarantine` states that it clears the observer only.
- Chronicle client packages are updated to 19.29.0.
