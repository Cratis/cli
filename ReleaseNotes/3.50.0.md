## Changed

- `cratis render` admits Screenplay ESM v9 and v10 models. Constructs the bundled Stage renderer does not support yet are refused with their own diagnostic instead of a generic version error: public events and event-target projections and reducers (`STAGE-ESM-032`), foreign origins and event-source captures (`STAGE-ESM-033`), reaction system identity (`STAGE-ESM-034`), reaction observer filters (`STAGE-ESM-035`) and per-production route overrides (`STAGE-ESM-036`). Reducer observer filters render. ESM v11 and later are still refused with `CLI-RENDER-004` (#303)
