## Summary

The CLI now consumes the published Generation 0.7 and Critter Stack 0.15 packages and reports application-owned Vogen evidence without confusing it with the bundled Cratis adapter.

## Added

- Report Vogen package, assembly, and value-object capability provenance independently from the Cratis adapter package. (#87)
- Admit exact Vogen 8.0.7 canonical evidence and fail closed for missing, malformed, divergent, unreviewed, or unsupported versions. (#87)
- Generate authored Vogen concepts and Critter Stack artifacts together through the default CLI facade. (#87)
- Count `VOG` diagnostics as lowering loss while retaining independent support and semantic-conformance dimensions. (#87)

## Changed

- Update bundled packages to Screenplay Generation 0.7.1 and Critter Stack 0.15.0. (#87)
- Document Vogen provenance, generated-source authority, and support boundaries. (#87)
