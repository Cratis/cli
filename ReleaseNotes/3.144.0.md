## Changed

- `cratis run` starts the Stage 6.4.0 container by default, and `cratis render` and `cratis screenplay` bundle Screenplay 6.37.0, Stage 6.4.0, the Stage Axon renderer 0.12.0 and the Arc Screenplay adapter 23.15.1 (#644)
- `cratis screenplay generate` with the `marten` and `critter-stack` providers runs the Critter Stack Screenplay adapter 0.29.1 and Screenplay Generation 0.24.0 (part of #638)
- Updated the CLI's other dependencies to their latest releases, including Chronicle 19.181.3, YamlDotNet 18.1.0 and System.Text.Json 11.0.0; `cratis prologue` stays on Prologue 2.1.0 for now (see https://github.com/Cratis/cli/issues/646)
