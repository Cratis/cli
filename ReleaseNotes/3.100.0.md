## Added

- `cratis ai test --install-only` runs the install-phase checks of AI corpus conformance fixtures without launching a harness, so the deterministic part of the suite can run in CI with no model quota. Each fixture runs for the harnesses it declares, or the configured ones when it declares none, and `--harnesses` narrows the selection. (part of #204)

## Changed

- `cratis ai test` reports a fixture check with an inconsistent shape as an error and names the reason, for example a `command` check without `run`, or a `command` check that also declares `path`, `glob` or `pattern`. (part of #204)
